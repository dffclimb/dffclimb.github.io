We present a proof‑of‑concept bionic hand connected to an edge server via 5G or Wi‑Fi. End‑to‑end measurements show that sensing, transmission, inference, and feedback can meet natural control latency targets (<125 ms) in realistic usage.

- Authors: Ozan Karaali, Hossam Farag, Strahinja Dosen, Cedomir Stefanovic
- Links: arXiv preprint - https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11729
