We outline a hierarchical architecture for bionic limbs that combines on-device control with edge/cloud offloading over reliable low‑latency 5G links. Time‑critical control stays local, while compute‑intensive perception and planning run on nearby edge or cloud servers.

- Authors: Ozan Karaali, Hossam Farag, Strahinja Dosen, Cedomir Stefanovic
- Venue: IEEE Internet of Things Magazine (Vol. 8, No. 4)
