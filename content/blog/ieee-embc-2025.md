Our article “Enabling Next‑Generation Cloud‑Connected Bionic Limbs Through 5G Connectivity” is out in IEEE Internet of Things Magazine. We detail a hierarchical architecture where time‑critical control stays on‑device while perception and planning are offloaded to edge/cloud over reliable low‑latency 5G links.

At IEEE EMBC 2025, we also presented “5G‑Enabled Smart Prosthetic Hand: Connectivity Analysis and Assessment”, sharing end‑to‑end latency measurements that meet natural control requirements in realistic scenarios.
